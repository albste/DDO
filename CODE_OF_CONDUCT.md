# Code of Conduct

This project asks for sharp criticism and respectful behaviour together.

## Expected

- Criticize ideas, mechanisms and documents, not people.
- Give reasons and, where you can, an alternative.
- Assume good faith and ask before assuming a motive.
- Welcome people of any background, language or level of expertise.
- Say clearly when you are unsure or speaking from a single jurisdiction.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sustained disruption, spam or off-topic promotion.
- Misrepresenting the status of the documents, for example claiming they are security-reviewed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may restrict participation of anyone who breaks these rules. To report a problem, write to stefano.albano.sa@gmail.com, or open an issue marked as conduct-related.

This code may change as the project grows.
