# Contributing

DDO is an open proposal. Criticism is as valuable as support. Start with [discussions/open-questions.md](discussions/open-questions.md) to see where help is most needed.

## How to take part

- **Critique:** open an issue describing the flaw (in the model, the incentives or the security assumptions), which document and section it concerns, and why it matters.
- **Propose:** open an issue or start a discussion with the alternative mechanism and your reasons. Say what problem it solves and what new risks it brings.
- **Test:** run part of the model in a simulation or a small group and share what happened, including failures.
- **Check legal fit:** describe the jurisdiction, the provision involved and how it interacts with the proposal.
- **Translate or clarify:** keep the numbered file names, one folder per language under `docs/`, and mark any wording you were unsure about.

## Ground rules

- Argue with reasons. Name the document and section you are discussing.
- Accepted changes are recorded in `discussions/decisions/` with their reasons, and listed in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- Do not present the technical specification as security-reviewed or suitable for binding elections.
- Be respectful: see [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
