# DDO: Direct Decentralized Organization

**English** | [Italiano](README.it.md)

An open proposal for a political system where citizens decide directly, power is decentralized and non-hierarchical, and influence follows verified competence within a fixed cap.

**Status:** draft v0.1. This is a proposal to be criticized and improved, not a finished system.

## The idea in brief

- Every citizen has the same baseline voice.
- Extra weight in a vote is earned only for competence and involvement in that specific domain, capped, non-transferable and fading over time.
- Representatives and public staff mediate and execute. They never decide.
- Identity and voting use self-sovereign identity, zero-knowledge proofs and verifiable voting, so the right to vote can be proven without revealing who you are, and anyone can check the results.
- A Charter of Fundamental Rights, outside ordinary votes, protects minorities.
- Adoption is staged: specification, prototype, community pilot, municipal pilot, parallel scale-up, then integration if citizens choose it.

## Honest limits

This project does not claim DDO is 100% feasible on paper. It declares risks, limits and open questions instead. The weakest point is weighted voting with strong privacy, which is still an open research problem. The technical specification is a design proposal and has not been security-reviewed. Do not use it for binding elections.

## How to contribute

Criticism is as valuable as support. You can:

- point out flaws in the model, incentives or security assumptions;
- propose alternatives to any mechanism, with reasons;
- test parts of the model in simulations or small groups;
- check the legal fit with a specific jurisdiction;
- translate or clarify the documents.

Open an issue or start a discussion. The contribution process may change as the project grows.
